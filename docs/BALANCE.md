# Fleet roles v0.1

Experimental and not yet playtested. These changes test fleet composition and upkeep tradeoffs; they are not claims of improved balance.

| Unit | Field | Original | Prototype | Reason |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Mon Calamari Cruiser | ShieldStrength | 300 | 400 | Strengthen its shield-focused role. |
| Mon Calamari Cruiser | MaintenanceCost | 70 | 74 | Pay additional upkeep for stronger shields. |
| Bulk Cruiser | MaintenanceCost | 30 | 24 | Offer a cheaper early line ship to sustain. |
| Assault Frigate | MaintenanceCost | 60 | 50 | Make an escort without fighter capacity less costly to sustain. |
| Nebulon-B Frigate | Hull | 600 | 700 | Give a vulnerable carrier escort modest extra durability. |
| Nebulon-B Frigate | MaintenanceCost | 35 | 32 | Encourage small mixed Rebel fleets. |
| Alliance Escort Carrier | MaintenanceCost | 29 | 25 | Reduce the cost of sustaining a fighter-centered fleet. |
| Corellian Corvette | MaintenanceCost | 23 | 18 | Make small escorts economical to retain. |
| Corellian Gunship | MaintenanceCost | 23 | 18 | Make dedicated escorts economical to retain. |
| Lancer Frigate | MaintenanceCost | 30 | 24 | Make fighter-screening escorts easier to include. |
| Imperial Star Destroyer | Hull | 2750 | 3100 | Increase staying power for the Imperial line ship. |
| Imperial Star Destroyer | MaintenanceCost | 71 | 80 | Charge additional upkeep for stronger hulls. |
| Imperial II Star Destroyer | Hull | 3000 | 3400 | Continue the durable Imperial line-ship identity. |
| Imperial II Star Destroyer | MaintenanceCost | 99 | 110 | Keep durability tied to a meaningful upkeep cost. |
| Super Star Destroyer | Hull | 5000 | 5500 | Give the flagship greater endurance. |
| Super Star Destroyer | MaintenanceCost | 170 | 190 | Keep flagship deployment economically consequential. |
| Imperial Escort Carrier | MaintenanceCost | 30 | 26 | Support carrier-based alternatives to destroyer-only fleets. |
| B-wing | MaintenanceCost | 8 | 6 | Reduce the upkeep penalty on a specialist bomber. |
| TIE Fighter | MaintenanceCost | 3 | 2 | Support the Empire's expendable fighter-screen role. |
| TIE Defender | RefinedMaterialCost | 5 | 6 | Give the advanced multirole fighter a greater production premium. |

This is a modest first pass at fleet roles and maintenance economics. Production speed, hyperdrive values, weapon formulas, AI, research progression, and character stats are unchanged. See [PLAYTESTING.md](PLAYTESTING.md) for the questions this preset is intended to investigate.
